## Findings

1. **#978 failed 1 of 7 container starts here** (step 2). 5 of the 7 starts showed
   `"8080/tcp": []` on their first `running` sample. Re-run a release after the #978 fix lands.
2. **The eval-only change re-evaluates the production image.** Step 8 warned
   `RELEASE_EVALSET_CHANGED` and said production "is running an image that failed the gate". That is
   correct: moving to another eval set does not change the image. Not a defect, but worth
   knowing: the console's gate strip still showed Production as passed after that run, with
   "local, ready". Per `docs/command-center.md`, Production comes from the production deployment,
   not the last eval run; that was not tested further here.
3. The skipped package version `0.1.0` (step 3) is the spec's reservation of a version by an
   interrupted release, not a finding.
