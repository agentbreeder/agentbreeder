# Northwind Pen (hardware accessory)

The Northwind Pen is a $79 smart stylus that captures handwriting into notes. It ships free to the US, Canada, the UK and the EU. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days; express (2 business days, $15) is available in the US.

Orders can be cancelled until they ship. Unused pens can be returned within 30 days for a full refund; the customer receives a prepaid return label by email. Defective pens are covered by a one-year warranty and are replaced free of charge.

Order status and tracking links are under Settings > Orders.
