# Billing and plans

Northwind Notes has three plans: Free ($0, 3 notebooks, 1 GB storage), Pro ($8 per user per month billed annually or $10 monthly, unlimited notebooks, 100 GB storage) and Team ($15 per user per month, adds shared workspaces, admin console and SSO).

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each billing cycle and are also available under Settings > Billing > Invoices. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Bank transfer is available for Team plans with 20 or more seats.

To change the billing contact or payment method, an account owner opens Settings > Billing > Payment method. Only account owners can change billing details.

Failed payments are retried after 3 and 7 days. After the third failure the workspace becomes read-only until payment succeeds; no data is deleted.

To cancel or downgrade a subscription, an account owner opens Settings > Billing > Change plan and chooses Cancel subscription or a lower plan. Monthly plans stay active until the end of the current billing period. For annual plans, the refund policy decides what is refunded. Data is kept; a cancelled workspace moves to the Free plan.
