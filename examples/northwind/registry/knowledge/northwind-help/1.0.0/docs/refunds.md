# Refund policy

Annual Pro and Team subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused full months when the customer downgrades or cancels.

Monthly plans are not refunded for the current month; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.

Duplicate charges are always refunded in full. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Support agents may approve refunds up to $200. Larger refunds, refunds outside this policy and any chargeback dispute must be escalated to the billing team.
