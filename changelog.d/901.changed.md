- Text that the CLI prints with visible escapes (`logs`, `services logs`, `invoke`, `evals`, `events`, `usage`) now also escapes invisible format characters: zero-width space, soft hyphen, BOM, U+2060–U+2064, U+206A–U+206F, the Mongolian vowel separator and the tag block U+E0000–U+E007F (shown as `\UXXXXXXXX`), so hidden text cannot ride along in a log line or reply. One-line fields replace them with a space. The set is the console's; zero-width joiner and non-joiner are kept (#901). On `main`, not in a published preview.
